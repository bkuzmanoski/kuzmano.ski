I’m **Brian Kuzmanoski**—a software designer and engineer with two decades of
[experience](/experience).

I love to [sweat the details](/work/kuzmano-ski-website). As AI makes software
easier to build, it’s the care that goes into design and implementation that
will [set great apps apart](/notes/building-software-with-ai).

<Aside label="Status">
  Available for product design and software engineering work, in Sydney or
  remotely.

  [Get in touch.](/contact)
</Aside>

**Recently**, I built [Traction’s macOS app](/work/traction): an AI notetaker
that detects meetings, transcribes calls in real time, and identifies speakers.
It pairs a native interface with an optimised audio pipeline, built on Core
Audio and Accelerate, that keeps CPU and battery usage low during long meetings.

**Previously**, I led product design at [Loopit](/work/loopit) and
[Kinesis](/work/kinesis), making complex software easy to use.

I do my best work with small, focused teams that move quickly and care about
what they make. If that’s how you work, [let’s talk](/contact).

_—BK_
