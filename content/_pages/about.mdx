I’m **Brian Kuzmanoski**—a software designer and engineer with two decades of
[experience](/experience).

I care deeply about craft and love to
[sweat the details](/work/kuzmano-ski-website). As AI makes software faster and
easier to build, [what sets great apps apart](/notes/building-software-with-ai)
will be how well they’re made.

<Aside label="Status">
  Available for product design or app development work, in Sydney or remotely.

  [Get in touch.](/contact)
</Aside>

**Recently**, I built [Traction’s macOS app](/work/traction): an AI notetaker
that detects meetings, transcribes calls in real time, and identifies speakers.
It pairs a native user interface with an efficient audio pipeline, built on Core
Audio and Accelerate, that minimises resource usage.

**Previously**, I led product design at [Loopit](/work/loopit) and
[Kinesis](/work/kinesis), working on intuitive user interfaces for complex
software products.

I do my best work with small, focused teams that value great user experience. If
that sounds like you, [let’s talk](/contact).

_**—BK**_
